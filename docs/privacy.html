Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Unchair is built to keep your data on your Mac.

What Unchair doesn't do

  • No accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking.
  • Unchair never sends your settings or history anywhere.

What stays on your Mac

  • Settings and history (focus sessions, sitting and standing time, water, and how you responded to reminders) are stored in Unchair's own app container on your Mac.
  • You can export your history (JSON or CSV) or delete it at any time in Settings → General.

What Unchair checks, and why

  • Keyboard and mouse activity: only how long ago you last used them, to notice when you're away. Unchair never sees what you type or click.
  • Microphone and camera: only whether another app is using them, to hold reminders during calls. Unchair never records, accesses, or shows audio or video.
  • Windows: only the size and owner of the frontmost app's windows, to notice full screen presentations. Unchair never reads window titles or contents.
  • Focus: if you add an Unchair Focus filter, macOS tells Unchair when that Focus turns on or off.

Network

  • Updates: Unchair checks lukazovic.github.io for new versions and downloads them from GitHub. These requests include the app and macOS versions.
  • Links: feedback, problem reports, and the website open in your browser on GitHub, where GitHub's privacy statement applies.
